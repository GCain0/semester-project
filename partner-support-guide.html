Partner Support Guide 💑

Partners play a crucial role in supporting the birthing parent before, during, and after labor. This guide provides clear, practical steps to help partners stay prepared, calm, and confident throughout the childbirth experience.

Before Labor

  • Prepare the hospital bag and double-check essentials.
  • Review the birth plan together and understand preferences.
  • Learn comfort techniques like breathing support and massage.
  • Keep important contacts ready, including family and medical providers.
  • Plan transportation and know the fastest route to the hospital.

During Labor

  • Stay calm and reassuring — your energy matters.
  • Offer physical comfort: hand-holding, massage, cool cloths, pillows.
  • Advocate respectfully by communicating the birth plan when needed.
  • Track contraction timing if early labor is at home.
  • Provide hydration and snacks when allowed.

After Delivery

  • Support immediate recovery with comfort and rest.
  • Assist with newborn care like diapering, swaddling, and soothing.
  • Manage communication with family so the birthing parent can rest.
  • Ensure the birthing parent eats and hydrates during recovery.
  • Be patient and encouraging as emotions fluctuate.
