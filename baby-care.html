Baby Care Basics 🧸

These baby care basics cover feeding, sleep, hygiene, and bonding tips to help new parents feel confident in daily routines.

Feeding

  • Feed on demand — watch hunger cues
  • Burp gently after each feeding
  • Keep bottles and nipples clean
  • Track feeding times and amounts

Sleep

  • Place baby on their back to sleep
  • Keep the sleep area clear of blankets and toys
  • Use a firm mattress and fitted sheet
  • Establish a calming bedtime routine

Hygiene & Comfort

  • Change diapers frequently
  • Use gentle baby soap and lotion
  • Keep nails trimmed safely
  • Dress baby in soft, breathable fabrics

Bonding

  • Hold and cuddle often
  • Talk and sing to your baby
  • Make eye contact and smile
  • Respond to cries with calm reassurance
