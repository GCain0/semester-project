Project Overview: Childbirth Preparation Website

Purpose

This website is designed to support expecting parents—especially me and my wife—as we prepare for labor, delivery, and the first days at home with our newborn. The goal is to create a calm, organized, and helpful resource that simplifies the overwhelming amount of information involved in childbirth preparation.

What the Project Is

The childbirth preparation website provides practical tools such as checklists, timelines, and guides for both birthing parents and partners. It offers clear, accessible information about what to pack, how to prepare, and what to expect. The site focuses on reducing stress and helping families feel confident and ready.

Why It Matters

Preparing for childbirth can be confusing and emotionally intense. This project matters because it organizes essential information into one supportive space. For me and my wife, it serves as a personalized resource we can rely on throughout our journey. For other expecting parents, it offers guidance that is simple, actionable, and reassuring.

Who It’s For

The website is intended for expecting parents, partners, and support people who want reliable, easy-to-understand preparation tools. It is especially helpful for first-time parents who may not know where to begin.

Project Goals

  • Provide clear checklists and preparation tools
  • Offer guidance for partners and support people
  • Create a calm, supportive design and user experience
  • Build interactive features such as checkable lists and timelines

Future Enhancements

  • Printable birth plans
  • Contraction timer
  • Newborn essentials guides
  • Expanded partner support resources
