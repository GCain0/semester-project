Newborn Essentials Checklist 🍼

These newborn essentials help you prepare for the first days and weeks at home with your baby. This list keeps things simple and focuses on what truly matters for comfort, safety, and care.

Clothing 👕

  • Onesies (short and long sleeve)
  • Sleepers and footed pajamas
  • Socks and mittens
  • Soft hats
  • Swaddle blankets

Feeding 🥣

  • Bottles (if bottle-feeding)
  • Formula (if needed)
  • Burp cloths
  • Nursing pillow (if breastfeeding)
  • Bottle brush and drying rack

Diapering 🚼

  • Newborn diapers
  • Wipes (sensitive skin recommended)
  • Diaper cream
  • Changing pad
  • Diaper pail or disposal bags

Bathing 🛁

  • Baby bathtub
  • Baby shampoo and wash
  • Soft towels
  • Washcloths
  • Baby lotion

Sleep Essentials 😴

  • Bassinet or crib
  • Firm mattress
  • Fitted sheets
  • White noise machine (optional)
  • Nightlight

Health & Safety ❤️

  • Baby thermometer
  • Nail clippers or file
  • Infant first-aid kit
  • Bulb syringe or nasal aspirator
  • Car seat (installed ahead of time)
