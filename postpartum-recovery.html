Postpartum Recovery Guide 🌸

This guide covers essential steps for physical and emotional recovery after childbirth, helping new parents heal, rest, and adjust to life with a newborn.

Physical Recovery

  • Rest as much as possible
  • Stay hydrated and eat nourishing meals
  • Follow postpartum care instructions from your provider
  • Use gentle movement and stretching

Emotional Wellbeing

  • Talk openly about feelings and experiences
  • Seek support from loved ones or professionals
  • Practice self‑care and patience
  • Recognize signs of postpartum depression early

Partner Support

  • Share responsibilities and rest time
  • Offer encouragement and reassurance
  • Help with meals, chores, and baby care
  • Listen and communicate with empathy
