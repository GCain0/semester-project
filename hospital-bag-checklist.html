Hospital Bag Checklist 👜

This checklist helps expecting parents pack everything needed for labor, delivery, and the first days with your newborn. It keeps things simple, organized, and stress‑free.

For the Birthing Parent

  • Comfortable robe or gown
  • Non-slip socks or slippers
  • Hair ties or headband
  • Chapstick (hospital air is dry)
  • Phone charger (extra-long cord recommended)
  • Toiletries (toothbrush, deodorant, shampoo)
  • Going-home outfit
  • Nursing bra (if planning to breastfeed)

For the Partner

  • Comfortable change of clothes
  • Snacks and drinks
  • Phone charger
  • Pillow or blanket
  • Toiletries
  • List of important contacts

For the Baby

  • Onesies and sleepers
  • Swaddle blanket
  • Baby hat and socks
  • Going-home outfit
  • Infant car seat (installed ahead of time)

Important Documents

  • IDs for both parents
  • Insurance card
  • Birth plan (optional)
  • Hospital paperwork (if pre-registered)

Optional Comfort Items

  • Portable speaker for calming music
  • Essential oils or diffuser
  • Favorite pillow
  • Tablet or book
